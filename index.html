How the free week works

Three steps, seven days, and a number at the end of it. Nothing to sign and nothing to install.

  1. Step 1

    Book the week

    Fifteen minutes on the phone to check your line is a fit, then we set it up. No new handsets, no downtime, and no change to how your team works.

  2. Step 2

    We count what you miss

    For seven days every unanswered call gets logged, and every caller gets a text asking what they needed. Their answers come straight to you.

  3. Step 3

    You see what it was worth

    You get the list, priced against your own average job. Then you know what the phone is costing you, and what it takes to stop it.

Then one of two things is true

The week does not just hand you a number, it tells you which problem you have got. They are different problems and they need different work.

Path one

The number is high

You are losing work that already found you. Every enquiry gets answered, qualified and logged, the quotes that go quiet get chased, and jobs go in the diary without anyone typing them out.

  • Call handling, including out of hours
  • Missed call follow up
  • Quote chasing and booking
Path two

The number is low

Then missed calls are not your problem, not enough people are ringing at all. That is a different job and it happens further up: being findable, and giving people a reason to pick up the phone.

  • A site built to turn a search into a call
  • Your Google listing and local search
  • Outbound to the customers you want more of

Either way you finish the week knowing which one you are, which is more than most people running a business can tell you. We will say which we would do in your position, and if the answer is nothing, we will say that too.
