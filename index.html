Straight answers

We already use a care system. Why this too?

Keep it. Care systems hold the young people’s records and treat the staff side as a bolt-on. Staffbook only does the staff side, so it goes deeper, and it sits beside your system rather than replacing it.

Who is behind this?

One person in Manchester, building it with a Responsible Individual who has run homes for twenty years. You talk to the person who built it.

Colby builds it, answers the emails and does the calls. colby@ordane.co.uk

What if you disappear?

Your data is never hostage. Everything exports to plain spreadsheets whenever you like. Worst case, you are back to Excel with better-organised files.

Is the data safe?

UK-hosted, every organisation walled off from every other at the database level, private files, append-only audit trail. The long version is written for your DPO.
