The problem

Most of it runs on someone remembering

“How many enquiries did you miss last week?”

Almost nobody can answer this. Not because they are careless, but because nothing keeps a record of the ones that got away.

“Who chases a quote that goes quiet?”

Usually whoever remembers. Which means the ones that get chased are the ones somebody happened to think of.

“How many places does that get typed?”

The same customer details go into the diary, the spreadsheet and the accounts package. Three times, by hand, every time.

“What is that costing you?”

This is the one that matters and it is never a number. It is a shrug. You cannot budget for a loss you have never seen measured.

How it works

We measure first, then we build

Nobody should buy a system on a hunch, including you. So the first thing we do is put a number on it.

  1. Step 1

    Fifteen minutes on the phone

    We work out how work actually reaches you and where it goes once it does. If there is nothing worth measuring, we will say so and that is the end of it.

  2. Step 2

    A week of measurement

    We put something light on top of what you already use. No new handsets, no migration, no downtime, nothing for your team to learn. It sits there for seven days and records what is falling through.

  3. Step 3

    You get the number

    At the end you get the list and what it was worth, priced against your own average job value. Not our benchmark, not an industry average. Yours.

  4. Step 4

    Then, and only then, we build

    The number tells us what to build. If enquiries are being dropped we fix the front door. If the admin is eating the week we automate it. If not enough is coming in at all, the problem was never the system and we say that too.

Reasonable questions

The things people ask first

Why is the first week free?

Because I would rather show you a number than argue with you about one. Most of this industry sells on a promise. The week means you decide with evidence off your own business, and if the number comes back small you have lost nothing and got an answer.

Do we have to change our phone system or software?

No. The measurement sits on top of what you already have. No porting, no new handsets, no migration, and nothing for your team to learn. If it needed all that to work, hardly anyone would let us try it.

Is a customer going to end up talking to a robot?

During the measurement week, no. Nothing answers on your behalf and your line behaves exactly as it does now. If we build call handling afterwards, how much it does and where it hands over to a person is your call, not mine.

What does the work cost after the week?

It depends entirely on what the number says needs building, so quoting before we have measured would be guesswork. You will get a fixed price before anything starts, and you are free to take the number and do nothing.

How new is this?

New. Ordane is founder led and I would rather tell you that than have you find out. What it means in practice is that you get me on everything, the measurement week costs you nothing, and I will turn down work I cannot do properly rather than learn on your money.

How long until it is doing something useful?

The measurement is live within about fifteen minutes and runs for seven days. A first build is usually a couple of weeks after that, depending on what it is.
