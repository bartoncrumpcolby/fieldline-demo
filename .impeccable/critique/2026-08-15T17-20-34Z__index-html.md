2. [P1] No closing CTA; the page ends on a shrug after the FAQ. Fix: short final band, one line + button. ($impeccable polish)
3. [P2] The explorer's 8 tabs are flat and over budget; strongest differentiators buried at the bottom. Fix: stage into labelled clusters (The records / The week / The proof). ($impeccable layout)
4. [P2] Half-implemented tab semantics (tablist without tab roles or arrow keys) and no styled :focus-visible. Fix: drop roles for aria-pressed, theme focus. ($impeccable audit)
5. [P3] Dead code: .seg/.views CSS+JS, .ministrip rules, .hero .grid duplicate, #start classed "price". ($impeccable polish)
