Measure. Diagnose. Fix.

The same three steps every time. Nothing gets built before we know it is worth building, and no lock in at any point.

  1. MeasureA week on your line, quietly. Every call that rings out gets logged and the caller gets asked what they needed.
  2. DiagnoseWe show you the numbers and tell you which problem you have got. Sometimes it is one thing, sometimes it is nothing, and we say so.
  3. FixWhatever you go ahead with is live within 48 hours. Changes, tuning and reporting stay ours. You are not handed a login and left to it.

The week tells you which one you are

Not an opinion about your phones. Real numbers off your own line, then a straight answer about what to do with them.

01

Every call you missed

Date, time and number for each one. Most people are surprised by the total and more surprised by when they happened.

02

What they actually wanted

Each one gets a text back asking what they need. Their answers come to you, so you know which were worth having.

03

What it was worth

We put your own average job value against the ones that were real work. That number is usually the reason people carry on.

Path one

The number is high

You are losing work that already found you. Every enquiry gets answered, qualified and logged, the quotes that go quiet get chased, and jobs go in the diary without anyone typing them out.

  • Call handling, including out of hours
  • Missed call follow up
  • Quote chasing and booking
Path two

The number is low

Then missed calls are not your problem, not enough people are ringing at all. That is a different job and it happens further up: being findable, and giving people a reason to pick up the phone.

  • A site built to turn a search into a call
  • Your Google listing and local search
  • Outbound to the customers you want more of

Either way you finish the week knowing which one you are, which is more than most people running a business can tell you. We will say which we would do in your position, and if the answer is nothing, we will say that too.
