WHY IT FEELS RIGHT

“The person testing the alarm at 2am is not a manager.”

Staffbook is not adapted from HR software. It was built alongside a serving Responsible Individual with twenty years in children’s homes — the screens answer what inspections actually ask, because she kept asking.

The rota, painted — not typed.

Pick a shift, click the cells. Hours count themselves, sleep-ins are marked, and any short day flags itself before the week starts.

Checks built around who actually does them.

Fridge temperatures to fire doors, waking night staff included, rota beside them.

No child records.
That is the point.

There is no field for a young person anywhere in Staffbook, so trying it never puts a child’s data anywhere new. It sits beside whatever care system the home already runs, holding the staff side only.
